# US Adjustable Wedding Welcome Sign Stand Copy Notes

Use for tall metal wedding welcome-sign holders, seating-chart stands, poster frames, and adjustable event display stands.

## Product-fact gate

Verify before drafting:

- Overall width/depth and every height setting in inches; do not mix `ft` and `meters`.
- Recommended sign dimensions for each height setting.
- Maximum tested sign thickness and weight, if any.
- Included frame sections, fasteners, strap count, strap material, colors, water-bag count, storage bag, instructions, and tools.
- Whether the sign board, printed poster, flowers, greenery, and décor are excluded.
- Finish/coating type and whether rust/corrosion claims have test support.
- Outdoor-use conditions: water-bag fill amount, surface, weather, wind, supervision, and storage.

If the BOM is unclear, write a conservative package statement and add a human confirmation checklist. Never convert ambiguous old wording such as `with 8 Leather and Water Bags` into a definite component claim without verification.

When multiple fields fail for `height_settings`, `base_dimensions`, `sign_thickness`, `included_water_bags`, or accessory-count ambiguity, apply `structured-fact-authorization-and-cascade-dedupe.md`: merge to root causes, authorize SKU facts first, then rewrite only citing fields. Do not treat bullet-by-bullet repeats of the same fact_key as independent copy defects.

## Market-language families

Useful buyer-language families to validate with Amazon autocomplete/SellerSprite/SIF:

- `wedding welcome sign stand`
- `wedding sign stand`
- `wedding sign holder`
- `adjustable wedding sign holder`
- `gold wedding sign stand`
- `tall wedding sign stand`
- `seating chart stand for wedding`
- `poster stand for wedding`
- `display easel`

A US autocomplete probe also surfaced modifiers such as `gold`, `tall`, `holder`, `6ft tall`, `72 inches`, `60 x 36`, and `rod pocket`. Treat these as shopper-language evidence only—not search-volume evidence and not automatic product facts. Exclude mechanism-specific or size-specific modifiers that do not match the SKU: a 68-inch strap-mounted frame must not inherit `6 ft`, `72 inches`, `60 x 36`, or `rod pocket` merely because autocomplete suggests them.

Separate `baby shower` and `bridal shower`; never write the malformed phrase `baby bridal shower`. When direct SERP results are blocked, autocomplete can support phrase selection, but it cannot support organic-rank, demand-tier, or competitor-performance claims.

## Copy allocation

- **Title:** brand + `Wedding Welcome Sign Stand` + adjustable/two-height mechanism + verified maximum dimensions + gold/metal + one distinct use phrase.
- **Item Highlights:** complementary package components, recommended sign sizes, stability mechanism, or representative events; do not repeat the title identity and dimensions mechanically.
- **Bullets:** finish/frame → two-height setup and recommended boards → weighted base/water bags → strap/package contents → events plus exclusions/conditions.
- **A+:** package checklist, assembly sequence, two-height diagram, recommended sign-size matrix, indoor/outdoor setup conditions, and event gallery.
- **Backend:** missing roots such as holder, seating chart, poster, display, reception, shower, graduation, entryway, event, foam board, acrylic, wooden sign only when verified.

## Short-field and bullet decision order

For the user's US short-title workflow:

- Produce three Title/Item Highlights options when requested: SEO/conversion balance, differentiating mechanism, and concise readability.
- Keep each title at 65–75 characters and lead with `Brand + Wedding Welcome Sign Stand`. Spend the remaining budget on one verified distinction such as gold metal, adjustable height, or a purchase-critical height range—not several event scenes or the full accessory list.
- Use Item Highlights for complementary structure and package clarity: strap count/colors, fillable water bags, base dimensions, recommended board materials, and `sign/decorations not included` when verified.
- If the title already contains adjustable height and dimensions, do not paraphrase them again in Item Highlights.

A useful buyer-decision order for this class is:

1. Height settings, footprint, and sign-layout guidance.
2. Frame material/finish, strap mechanism, and compatible board materials.
3. Base/water-bag mechanism, level-surface requirement, and outdoor limitations.
4. Concrete display tasks: seating charts, directional boards, shower messages, graduation posters, or retail announcements.
5. Package contents, assembly, exclusions, and pre-display connection checks.

Use 3–7 word Title Case bullet headings. When the user requests the detailed format, show a planning table first, then English/Chinese copy, intent, keywords, deterministic character/word counts, overlap/compliance checks, and a final upload-only English bullet block.

## Competitor evidence boundaries

Same-form competitors may differentiate with digital design bundles, ground stakes, adjustable feet, backup straps, installation videos, or different strap counts. These are competitor mechanisms, not own-product facts. Use them to identify buyer questions and positioning gaps only after checking the own BOM.

If competitor review bodies are unavailable, do not label inferred concerns as negative-review findings. Repeated competitor emphasis on sign size/thickness, stability, included straps, outdoor setup, and assembly may support an `inferred buyer questions` section, clearly labeled as such.

## Claim guardrails

Avoid without independent evidence:

- `rust-proof`, `anti-rust`, `weatherproof`
- `wind-resistant`, `stable in any wind`
- `heavy duty`
- `30 lb capacity` or other load limits
- `fits any size sign`
- `securely holds` every board material/thickness
- `long-term outdoor use`

Use qualified alternatives when supported by the design:

- `glossy gold finish`
- `wide rectangular base`
- `fillable water bags help improve stability`
- `recommended for signs up to ...` only after testing/verification
- `designed for indoor events and supervised outdoor setups`
- `helps resist tipping when properly assembled and weighted`

## Image audit and redesign traps

- Main image should show only included components on pure white. Remove printed signs, flowers, greenery, candles, and event décor unless included.
- If lifestyle images show a finished sign, add `Sign and decorations not included` where readable.
- Do not show an outdoor wedding scene with unconditional `Stable Outdoor Use` or `Rust-Proof` badges.
- Dimension graphics must use one unit system consistently and distinguish frame size from recommended sign size.
- Show the exact number of straps and water bags; avoid ambiguous visual grouping.
- Comparison graphics must not make unsupported competitor disparagement such as `too many screws make installation difficult`.
- Replace machine-translated image copy (`not easy to dump`, `elebater styling`) with native US phrasing.
- A package-content image, two-height assembly diagram, and recommended-board matrix usually answer more conversion objections than another decorative event collage.

## Review and funnel interpretation

A mature scene stack can look attractive while still losing CTR/CVR through a noncompliant main image, ambiguous components, unsupported outdoor claims, and dimension confusion. Separate these from price, rating, review count, Buy Box, and traffic breadth. When backend metrics are unavailable, label the diagnosis as public-page evidence rather than a measured CTR/CVR conclusion.
