# US Small Mesh Zipper Pouches

Use this reference for US bill-size PVC mesh zipper pouches, pencil pouches, board-game component bags, and sibling pack-count variations.

## Product-fact gate

Verify each child separately before drafting:

- Selected child ASIN and parent ASIN.
- Pack count; do not inherit `30 Pack` into 45- or 60-piece siblings.
- Number of colors and whether the quantity is evenly distributed by color.
- Exact external dimensions and whether they are bill size, A6, pencil size, or mixed size.
- Material (`PVC mesh`, polyester mesh, fabric, etc.), translucency, zipper type, and label pocket if present.
- Supported use objects: bills, receipts, coupons, game pieces, office supplies, cosmetics, craft items, travel accessories.
- Water claim evidence. A splash-resistant surface does not support `waterproof`.

## Short Title allocation

For the user's US short-field workflow, normally use:

`Pack Count + Core Product Phrase + Verified Size/Format + Secondary Storage Phrase`

Useful market-language families:

- `small mesh zipper pouches`
- `mesh zipper pouch`
- `small zipper pouch`
- `bill size storage bags`
- `pencil pouch` / `pencil bags` only when the product form and own-page evidence support that identity
- `zipper pouches for organizing`

Keep the strongest natural phrase intact. Do not chain every synonym. Child-specific pack count and purchase-critical pouch size can stay in the title; omit brand when the user's active batch format says no brand.

## Item Highlights allocation

Default for this user's workflow: **do not repeat dimensions in Item Highlights**. Use the field for:

`Material/visibility + color assortment + buyer task/storage objects + representative scenarios`

Preferred shape:

`Translucent PVC mesh pouches in [verified color count] for bills, board-game pieces and office supplies at home, school or while traveling`

Keep it one coherent phrase under 125 characters. Capitalize meaningful product/feature words but leave ordinary connectors (`with`, `for`, `and`, `at`, `or`, `in`, `the`) lowercase unless phrase-initial.

## Claim guardrails

- Replace unsupported `waterproof` with `water-resistant surface`, `helps protect from light splashes`, or omit the claim.
- Do not claim leakproof, food-safe, archival-safe, tearproof, or child-safe without evidence.
- Do not import a competitor's label pocket, reinforced seams, zipper material, or mixed-size assortment.
- `Board game storage` is acceptable only when the pouch size and own listing support game-piece organization; do not imply a whole boxed game fits.

## Parent/child handling

Build a compact matrix for pack-count siblings. Keep shared material and use cases family-safe, but map pack count, dimensions, color count, and per-color quantity to each child. Search visibility is child-level; do not treat one sibling's public result position as the parent's rank.

## Verification

- Title within the active character target and no exact token appears more than twice.
- Item Highlights within 125 characters and contain no dimensions unless explicitly requested.
- Highlight capitalization is natural, not every-word Title Case.
- No unsupported `waterproof` claim.
- Draft is labeled for the exact child pack count.
