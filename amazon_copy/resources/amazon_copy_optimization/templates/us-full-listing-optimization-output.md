# US Listing Optimization Deliverable Template

Use when the user explicitly requests a full US listing optimization with short titles, COSMO/Rufus intent mapping, detailed bullet diagnostics, and upload-ready output.

## Evidence header

- Marketplace, brand, ASIN, product type
- Public PDP facts and timestamp/snapshot label
- Keyword evidence source: SellerSprite/SIF/ABA, Amazon autocomplete, or one-time public SERP
- Competitor evidence source and limitations
- Explicitly separate verified facts, old-copy claims, competitor claims, and `待确认` facts

## 1. Product fact and conflict table

| Field | Verified value | Source | Status/risk |
|---|---|---|---|

Cover dimensions, count, material, color, package components, assembly, compatibility, exclusions, maintenance, safety limits, and parent/child scope.

## 2. Positioning and purchase-decision analysis

- Core positioning
- Target users
- Used For / Used In / Works With
- Top five purchase decisions in priority order
- Buyer concerns and likely return causes
- Own advantages vs limitations

Do not call inferred buyer questions “review pain points” unless review bodies were actually retrieved. When only competitor bullets are available, label them as repeated market objections or inferred questions.

## 3. Keyword classification and allocation

Classify core, secondary, high-intent, long-tail, attribute, material, structure, function, scenario, audience, pain-point, compatibility, synonym, competitor-language, and prohibited/inapplicable terms.

If numeric search volume/CVR is unavailable, label phrases as autocomplete-supported, repeated market language, or high-intent candidates—not verified high-volume/high-conversion terms.

Map terms to Title, Item Highlights, Bullet 1–5, Product Description, and backend Search Terms before drafting.

## 4. Three Title + Item Highlights variants

Produce:

- A: SEO and conversion balance
- B: Differentiation/mechanism emphasis
- C: Concise readability

For each show:

- Title
- Title character count
- Item Highlights
- Item Highlights character count
- Main keyword coverage

For the user's US short-field format, Title must be 65–75 characters and never exceed 75; Item Highlights must not exceed 125. Keep the core product identity near the front. Do not fill unused characters with secondary scenes or an entire package list. Programmatically verify exact-token repetition and UTF-8 bytes.

Recommend one pair and audit Title/Highlight semantic overlap, missing critical specs, prohibited symbols, unsupported claims, and package ambiguity.

## 5. Bullet planning table

| Bullet | Main buyer question | Core information | Primary keyword theme | Repetition to avoid |
|---|---|---|---|---|

Adapt the order to the product. Compatibility, load, dimensions, safety, or package exclusions move earlier when they are the main purchase gate.

## 6. Detailed bullets

For each Bullet Point 1–5 output:

- English copy
- Chinese translation
- Main purchase intent
- Covered keywords
- English character count
- English word count

Use a natural Title Case subheading of 3–7 words followed by a colon. Target about 210–300 characters and 32–50 words, but stop earlier when the buyer question is fully answered. One buyer intent per bullet; feature must lead to mechanism/benefit plus any necessary limitation.

## 7. Supporting fields

- Product Description
- Backend Search Terms, lowercase and space-separated, ≤249 UTF-8 bytes
- COSMO/Rufus question-to-field map when requested
- Human confirmation checklist

## 8. Final checks

Report:

- Bullet overlap
- Scenario-listing risk
- Feature vs final-use separation
- Keyword stuffing
- Native-US readability
- Unsupported use/performance claims
- Dimension/count/package ambiguity
- Maintenance, installation, compatibility, or safety omissions

## 9. Upload-ready block

End with a clean block containing only the five English bullets. Keep analysis, translations, counts, and notes outside that block.
