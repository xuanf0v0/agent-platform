# COSMO and Alexa for Shopping: Evidence and Boundaries

## Terminology

- `Alexa for Shopping` is the current name from May 13, 2026.
- `Rufus` is the former name and still appears in older Seller Central materials and user prompts.
- Keep both terms in triggers, but use `Alexa for Shopping（原 Rufus）` in current deliverables.

## What Amazon publicly supports

Amazon Science describes COSMO as a large-scale e-commerce commonsense knowledge generation and serving system. It mines user-centric commonsense relationships from behavior, uses LLM-generated assertions refined with human-in-the-loop data, covers 18 major categories, and has been deployed in Amazon search applications such as search navigation.

Official source:

- https://www.amazon.science/publications/cosmo-a-large-scale-e-commerce-common-sense-knowledge-generation-and-serving-system-at-amazon

Amazon says Alexa for Shopping can answer questions, compare and recommend products, and draw from Amazon's catalog, reviews, community Q&A, customer activity, conversational context, and information across the web.

Official source:

- https://www.aboutamazon.com/news/retail/amazon-rufus-ai-assistant-personalized-shopping-features

Amazon's seller guidance says clear listing content and complete, accurate attributes help the shopping assistant answer common buyer questions and make recommendations.

Official source:

- https://sellercentral.amazon.com/seller-forums/discussions/t/ba85d226-ac00-4f84-9292-27e65379c4cc

## Operational inference allowed

The listing can improve semantic answerability by connecting:

`task or scenario → verified attribute → mechanism → benefit → limitation or fit`

Useful surfaces:

- Title for identity and decisive fit.
- Item Highlights for complementary material/use/comparison details.
- Bullets for five buyer decisions.
- Structured attributes for exact facts.
- Images/video for visual proof.
- Description/A+ for objections, setup, care, and own-product comparison.
- Search Terms for relevant residual vocabulary.

Use reviews, Q&A, SQP, autocomplete, related searches, and customer-service questions to discover natural language. Preserve source labels and time windows.

## Claims not supported by public evidence

Do not claim:

- A fixed keyword count, root count, or intent-term density improves COSMO ranking.
- Repeating a term across every field guarantees indexing or recommendation.
- Image filenames, Posts cadence, or a fixed weekly Q&A volume is a ranking factor.
- A+ is absolutely indexed or absolutely not indexed in all contexts.
- A listing alone controls Alexa for Shopping answers.
- A generated TOP20 intent list represents actual user shares.
- An invented percentage is market research.
- COSMO or Alexa for Shopping guarantees rank, traffic, conversion, or ad performance.

## Answerability audit

For each important buyer question:

1. Record the question in marketplace-native language.
2. Record the verified answer and source.
3. Identify the best listing field.
4. Check whether copy, attribute, and image agree.
5. Mark missing evidence rather than drafting around it.

Score separately:

- Policy compliance.
- Fact completeness/consistency.
- Query relevance backed by data.
- Buyer-question coverage.
- Readability and localization.
- Claim risk.

Never merge these dimensions into an opaque “COSMO score.”
