# Kids Party Favors / Mini Squishy Toys Reference

Use this reference when creating US Amazon listing copy for bulk kids' mini squishy toys, soft rubber squeeze toys, or party favor toy packs.

## Confirmed pattern from session

Common product setup:
- Category: Toys / Party Supplies
- Product naming: prefer generalized terms such as `Mini Squishy Toys`, `Soft Rubber Squishy Toys`, `Mini Squeeze Toys`
- Avoid over-specific product names like `Mochi Squishy Toys` unless the user explicitly confirms that wording
- Materials may be `soft rubber`; do not upgrade this into unsupported `non-toxic`, `100% safe`, or similar claims
- Age marking may be `3+`; phrase as `Recommended for ages 3+` or `Ages 3+`
- If the user provides CPC qualification, phrase as `CPC certified` or `CPC-certified for children’s product compliance`

## Buyer intent map

| Intent | Copy angle | Safe wording |
|---|---|---|
| Birthday party favors | Small toys for party bags and return gifts | `party favors for kids`, `birthday party favors`, `goodie bag fillers` |
| Classroom rewards | Bulk reward-bin items for teachers | `classroom prizes`, `treasure box toys`, `reward toys` |
| Holiday fillers | Small gifts for seasonal use | `Easter egg fillers`, `stocking stuffers`, `Valentine gifts` |
| Non-candy favors | Alternative to candy-only bags | `small toys instead of candy-only party favors` |
| Sensory/fidget play | Squeeze/hold/stretch tactile play | `fidget-style squeeze toys`, `playful sensory moment` |
| Trust/compliance | Parent/teacher confidence | `CPC certified`, `recommended for ages 3+`, `follow package age guidance` |

## Title guidance

Default short-title pattern:

`Brand + Count + Mini Squishy Toys + for Kids Party Favors`

Examples:
- `DALTACK 96 PCS Mini Squishy Toys for Kids Party Favors`
- `SUPEASY 198 PCS Mini Squishy Toys for Kids Party Favors`

If the user has two brands, produce parallel title variants per brand instead of mixing brands in one title.

## Bullet ordering

Recommended 5-bullet sequence:

1. `BULK MINI SQUISHIES` — count, bulk value, parents/teachers/party planners
2. `READY FOR PARTY BAGS` — goodie bags, treasure boxes, classroom prizes, carnival rewards, piñata fillers, Easter egg fillers, stocking stuffers
3. `SOFT RUBBER FEEL` — material and tactile squeeze/stretch/hold experience; avoid unsupported safety claims
4. `FUN LITTLE REWARDS` — emotional and use-case value; non-candy party favor alternative
5. `CPC CERTIFIED, AGES 3+` — CPC + age marking + supervised-use wording

## Compliance guardrails

Allowed when supported by user brief:
- `made with soft rubber`
- `CPC certified`
- `recommended for ages 3+`
- `designed for children’s party and reward use`
- `use under adult supervision as needed`

Avoid unless backed by explicit test reports or required label text:
- `100% safe`
- `non-toxic`
- `risk-free`
- `choking hazard free`
- `safe for all ages`
- `for babies`
- `for toddlers under 3`

If the item is a children's toy, explicitly flag that CPC files, package age marking, and warning language should be manually reviewed before upload.

## Search terms

Safe backend search term pattern:

`mini squishies soft rubber toys squeeze toys treasure box toys classroom prizes carnival prizes pinata fillers easter egg fillers stocking stuffers kids birthday gifts sensory toys reward toys small toys bulk`

Do not include competitor/brand terms such as `Needoh`, `Nee Doh`, `Play-Doh`, or other trademarked phrases unless the user owns the mark and explicitly instructs use.
