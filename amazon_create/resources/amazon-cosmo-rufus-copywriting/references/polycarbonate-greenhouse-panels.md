# Polycarbonate Greenhouse Panels / Sheets — US Listing Notes

Use for US Amazon copywriting when the product is polycarbonate greenhouse panels, twin-wall sheets, clear roof panels, greenhouse replacement panels, patio/canopy/carport cover panels, or similar outdoor DIY roofing sheets.

## Buyer-intent priorities

1. **Size and pack fit** — buyer needs the right panel size and quantity for a frame, roof, wall, cold frame, canopy, carport, or patio cover.
2. **6mm / 0.24 in thickness** — important spec; include in Title when space allows, especially under the 75-character title rule.
3. **Twin-wall structure** — high-value structural term for insulation and product identification.
4. **Greenhouse roof/wall replacement** — high-intent replacement use case; include in Item Highlights or Bullet 1.
5. **Light transmission + UV protection** — write as clear panels allowing sunlight through and UV-protected material helping reduce outdoor aging/yellowing/brittleness. Avoid absolute claims.
6. **Weather-ready durability** — impact-resistant polycarbonate, rain/wind/temperature swing exposure; avoid hail-proof, unbreakable, or indestructible unless test data is supplied.
7. **Cut-to-fit DIY** — include trimming/custom sizing plus safety note: measure carefully; use proper tools, gloves, and eye protection.

## Title pattern under 75 characters

Preferred formula:

`Brand + Pack + Size in inches + 6mm Twin-Wall Greenhouse Panels`

Examples:

- `RITOLLO 4 Pack 47.2" x 23.6" 6mm Twin-Wall Greenhouse Panels`
- `RITOLLO 14 Pack 94.5" x 47.2" 6mm Twin-Wall Greenhouse Panels`

This pattern puts the critical fit information, thickness, twin-wall structure, and core product term into the short title.

## Item Highlights pattern

Use Item Highlights to carry material/structure and replacement/application intent:

`0.24 in / 6mm twin-wall clear panels for greenhouse roof replacement, wall repair, cold frames, canopies and patio covers`

Keep under 125 characters and readable; do not keyword-stuff.

## Bullet ordering

1. **RIGHT SIZE OPTIONS** — size/pack/thickness + replacement/wall repair/cold frame/garden build intent.
2. **TWIN-WALL WARMTH** — hollow twin-wall polycarbonate sheets, reduce heat loss, lightweight handling, steady growing space.
3. **CLEAR UV PROTECTION** — sunlight for plants + UV-protected material helps resist yellowing/brittleness/outdoor aging.
4. **WEATHER-READY BUILD** — impact-resistant polycarbonate panels, rain/wind/temperature swings, better than thin film covers only if framed as practical comparison, not absolute proof.
5. **CUT-TO-FIT DIY** — trim plastic roofing sheets for greenhouses/canopies/carports/patio covers; include safety note.

## Safe claim language

Prefer:

- `helps reduce heat loss`
- `helps resist yellowing, brittleness and outdoor aging`
- `impact-resistant polycarbonate panels`
- `stand up to rain, wind, temperature swings and daily outdoor use`
- `better than thin film covers` (only as a practical/relative statement; avoid overclaiming)
- `trim to fit` / `cut-to-fit DIY`

Avoid unless substantiated:

- `unbreakable`
- `shatterproof`
- `hail proof`
- `100% UV proof`
- `never yellow`
- `hurricane proof`
- `waterproof forever`
- `indestructible`

## Readability / scoring lessons

For this class, if a review or scoring pass says the copy is too long, compress bullets to roughly 150–180 characters each when possible and keep total bullet text around or under ~1000 characters. Keep lead-in labels short, clear, and buyer-oriented. Reinforce `6mm`, `twin-wall`, `greenhouse replacement`, `roofing sheets`, `clear panels`, `weather-ready`, and `seedlings` where relevant without stuffing.
