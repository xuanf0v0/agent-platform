# US Outdoor Bird Bath Short-Field Copy

Use this reference for Amazon US metal bird baths, freestanding birdbaths, outdoor bird water stations, and bird-bath/feeder combinations.

## Product-fact gate

Verify before drafting:

- Saleable child vs parent ASIN and selected color/style.
- Overall height vs stake/pole height; never call total height the stand height unless verified.
- Bowl diameter, depth, and stated capacity.
- Material and coating; qualify rust/weather claims unless supported by product data or testing.
- Base mechanism: prong count, base width, and insertion design.
- Whether the bowl is removable and whether feeding surfaces are actually included.
- Whether a solar fountain is included or merely compatible; never imply inclusion when it is not.
- Assembly method and included hardware.

Do not import competitor dimensions, capacity, prong count, coating performance, stability, or included parts.

## Market-language families

Public competitor wording commonly includes:

- `metal bird bath for outside`
- `bird baths for outdoors`
- `garden bird bath`
- `freestanding birdbath`
- `bird feeder`
- `bird bath bowl`
- `outdoor bird water station`

Treat these as market-language evidence only unless SellerSprite/SIF/SQP confirms search volume or traffic contribution.

## Short-title allocation

When the user requests the current short-field format, keep the title within the requested/team cap and preserve one clear identity phrase. Useful structures include:

- `Height + Metal Bird Bath for Outside + Bowl Diameter/Capacity + Bird Feeder`
- `Metal Bird Bath for Outside + Verified Height + Bowl Size + Feeder Function`

Dimensions and capacity may appear in the title when the user explicitly permits them and they materially differentiate the item. Prioritize:

1. Overall height when verified.
2. Bowl diameter or capacity when purchase-relevant.
3. Dual bird-bath/feeder function when true.

Do not crowd the title with every measurement, base dimension, color, four room/yard scenarios, gift holidays, or unsupported adjectives. Remove seasonal gift phrases such as `Father's Day` from evergreen titles unless the user specifically requests a seasonal campaign field.

If the user requests a keyword-only title, omit brand/specifications and move all verified dimensions, capacity, color, material, and base details to Item Highlights. Do not assume this override applies to every ASIN.

## Item Highlights allocation

Item Highlights should read as one coherent native phrase, not comma-joined Search Terms. Use the remaining verified facts to complement the title:

`Color/style + freestanding birdbath synonym + bowl depth/removability + base mechanism + 2–3 outdoor scenarios`

Good pattern:

`Vintage-style freestanding birdbath with a removable deep bowl and a multi-pronged base for gardens, patios and yards`

When exact numbers matter, retain units clearly and distinguish diameter from depth. Avoid repeating all title measurements unless the repeated noun is needed to explain a different property such as `removable bowl` or `2-inch deep bowl`.

## Claim guardrails

- Prefer `rust-resistant coating` over `rust-proof` unless testing supports the absolute claim.
- Prefer `helps reduce tipping` or `supports stability` over `windproof`, `tip-proof`, or `superior stability` without comparative evidence.
- Avoid `perfect for all birds`, `attracts birds`, or guaranteed wildlife outcomes.
- `Heavy-duty`, `weatherproof`, and durability claims require support; do not inherit them from competitor wording.
- If a solar fountain is not included, state `solar fountain not included` wherever compatibility is mentioned.
- Gift occasions are secondary intent, not core title identity.

## Final checks

- Title and Highlights map to the correct child/style.
- Total height, bowl diameter, depth, and capacity are not conflated.
- Bird-feeder language is used only when the product provides a verified feeding surface.
- Prong count/base width match the product, not a competitor.
- Color and outdoor scenarios are placed in Highlights when title space is better spent on identity and quantified differentiation.
- Character counts, UTF-8 bytes, and exact-token repetition are verified deterministically.
