## Claim guardrails

Do not borrow competitor claims for baking, cooking, serving food, food safety, no chemicals, no coating/dyes, child safety, smooth edges, or breakage protection. Natural-shell food-contact claims require independent documentation and human compliance review. Use natural variation and careful-handling language to set expectations without promising identical shape, color, texture, or edges.

Open-flame imagery is a separate proof gate: shell candles or a flame burning directly inside a shell imply heat/fire suitability. Remove those scenes unless the product has documented evidence for that use and the final presentation includes appropriate safety context. Likewise, `round and smooth`, `safe`, and `smell-free` are not suitable defaults for variable natural shells.

Keep finished-project inspiration in secondary images, and make the DIY context unmistakable. If examples require holes, glue, paint, jewelry findings, stands, wax, or other supplies that are not included, avoid compositions that imply those components arrive in the package.

## Vase-filler and structured-attribute traps

For large 4–5-inch shells listed under `Vase Fillers`, do not let craft copy erase the category use entirely. Explain only verified display tasks such as arranging in wide-mouth vases, bowls, shadow boxes, or coastal displays; do not promise compatibility with narrow containers. Keep `vase filler seashells` as a useful autocomplete/category phrase when the form factor fits.

Audit structured attributes as well as visible copy:

- A PDP may expose `Model Name` or `Set Name` containing `Craft Kit` even when the package includes shells only. Flag this for human correction; never repeat `kit` unless tools and accessories are actually included.
- A generic `Item dimensions L x W x H` value may describe a representative shell or catalog field, not a guaranteed dimension for every natural piece. Prefer `approximately 4–5 inches across` and natural-variation language instead of presenting three fixed dimensions.
- User-provided weight must be classified as net product weight, packaged weight, or shipping weight before entering visible copy. Weight is usually lower priority than count and approximate size for this class.
- Category rank and family-shared reviews belong to the selected child/public variation context, not automatically to the parent as a saleable product.

## Image and DIY expectation checks for large shells

- A main image showing nine raw shells plus one painted shell and a hand may visually total ten pieces while still misrepresenting package contents. Count accuracy does not cure a main-image violation: show only the included raw shells on white and move painted examples/hands to secondary images.
- `Easy to grab`, `Round and smooth`, `Flat`, and `Clear texture` are weak or misleading defaults for naturally curved, ridged shells. Use `natural ridges`, `curved shell shape`, `front and back views`, and `may vary slightly` when supported.
- A secondary image with a flame burning directly inside a shell is a heat/open-flame suitability claim even if the text only says `DIY` or `Decoration`. Remove candle scenes unless documented heat/fire evidence and safety context exist.
- Jewelry, wind chimes, ornaments, wreaths, drilled hangings, resin pieces, display stands, paint, glue, beads, wax, and cords are finished-project inspiration. State that accessories are not included and avoid implying that shells arrive drilled, painted, polished, coated, or ready for a specific chemical process.
