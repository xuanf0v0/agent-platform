# US Short-Field Office Organizer Examples

Use these examples when drafting US office-storage listings under the team's short-field format: Title ≤75 characters, Item Highlights ≤125 characters, backend Search Terms ≤249 UTF-8 bytes.

## 1. Item Highlights must read naturally

Rejected shape:

`Desk organizer, pencil holder, pen holder, metal mesh, tilted slots...`

Why it fails: it is Search Terms with punctuation. It technically covers keywords but reads stiffly and lowers perceived quality.

Preferred shape:

`Metal mesh pencil holder and desk organizer with tilted slots for easy access to stationery at home, school or office`

Pattern:

`material + naturally embedded core product phrase + structure/benefit + 2–3 representative use cases`

Use only one or two compatible keyword phrases. Do not force every synonym into the visible field.

## 2. Pack-count compression

When a 1 Pack title already consumes 75 characters and a 2 Pack child must be created, preserve the defining mechanism and core product phrase; move a secondary modifier elsewhere.

Example:

- 1 Pack: `ReePlan Expandable Desk Drawer Organizer, 10 Adjustable Compartments, Black`
- 2 Pack: `ReePlan 2 Pack Expandable Desk Drawer Organizer, 10 Compartments, Black`

Keep `Expandable` because it defines the product and matches high-intent market language. Move `Adjustable` to backend Search Terms or explain adjustability through removable dividers in Item Highlights.

For multi-pack Highlights, add `Each` when a count/spec applies per unit:

`Each metal mesh drawer organizer has 5 removable dividers...`

Without `Each`, buyers may interpret five dividers as the total across the 2 Pack.

## 3. Mandatory keyword semantic safety

A user-supplied must-keep keyword can be relevant for indexing but misleading as visible copy. Example: `magnetic folders` for a product that is a magnetic file holder. Writing the phrase visibly can imply that the folders themselves are magnetic.

Safe allocation:

- Title: `magnetic file folder holders`
- Item Highlights: `magnetic file holder`
- Backend: add the missing root `folders`

Explain that Amazon can combine tokens across fields. Preserve the search intent without changing the product identity.

## 4. Rebuild Search Terms after every visible-field change

Whenever Title or Item Highlights changes:

1. Normalize visible-field tokens and basic singular/plural stems.
2. Remove newly covered backend words.
3. Return displaced useful roots if a title modifier was removed for pack/count compression.
4. Add only verified object types, tasks, or scenarios from the own PDP, direct competitor, or relevant SERP language.
5. Recount UTF-8 bytes; keep ≤249.

Do not fill remaining bytes with weak or unsupported terms merely to reach 249.
