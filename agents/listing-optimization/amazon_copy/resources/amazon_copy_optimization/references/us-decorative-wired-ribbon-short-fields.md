# US Decorative Wired Ribbon Short Fields

Use this reference for US decorative ribbon, velvet wired ribbon, Christmas ribbon, wreath ribbon, gift-wrap ribbon, and seasonal craft-ribbon ASINs under the short-field workflow.

## Product-fact gate

Verify from the selected child or user materials:

- width and continuous length (`4 in × 15 yd`, etc.);
- color/finish wording (burgundy, metallic gold, crushed velvet effect);
- material (`velvet`, fabric blend if disclosed);
- wired-edge construction;
- whether the roll is continuous or cut pieces;
- pack/roll count;
- recommended uses and occasions.

Do not infer color effects from a tiny/placeholder image. When the public image stack is missing or low resolution, treat finish language as verified only if the current child title, structured fields, packaging, or user material states it. Avoid `fade resistant`, `weatherproof`, `washable`, `premium`, exact wire gauge, or guaranteed bow-holding claims without evidence.

## Title allocation

For a brandless keyword-dense child title, prefer:

`Width + Color/Finish + Material/Construction + Seasonal Product Phrase + Length`

Example pattern only:

`4-Inch Burgundy Red Gold Crushed Velvet Wired Christmas Ribbon, 15 Yards`

- Preserve one natural high-intent construction such as `velvet wired ribbon` or `wired Christmas ribbon`.
- Keep width and continuous length when they drive purchase choice.
- Do not repeat `ribbon` more than twice merely to stack `craft ribbon`, `Christmas ribbon`, and `wreath ribbon`.
- Put secondary tasks and occasions into Item Highlights or backend terms.

## Item Highlights allocation

Use one complementary phrase that adds a distinct keyword family plus verified tasks:

`Continuous Craft Ribbon with [verified finish] for Gift Wrapping, Trees, Wreaths, Bows, Sewing and Holiday Decorations`

- Keep connectors (`with`, `for`, `and`, `at`, `or`, `in`) lowercase when the active batch uses meaningful-word capitalization.
- Treat `bow`, `tree`, `wreath`, `gift wrapping`, `sewing`, `scrapbooking`, and `upholstery` as use cases, not performance promises.
- If the field exceeds 125 characters, preserve the core product phrase, verified finish/construction, and top purchase tasks; remove the lowest-priority occasion/object first.

## Backend Search Terms

After final visible fields, add only missing roots from verified or strongly relevant intent families:

- floral: `floral arrangement garland centerpiece florist bouquet`;
- occasions: `anniversary birthday easter halloween seasonal festive`;
- crafts: `scrapbooking upholstery trim embellishment diy`;
- packaging/display: `roll spool present packaging mantle staircase`.

Do not use competitor brands, unsupported fabric types, `waterproof`, `outdoor`, `fadeproof`, or weak filler. Keep terms lowercase, space-separated, deduplicated, and ≤249 UTF-8 bytes.

## New-listing evidence limitation

When SellerSprite returns null title/keyword rows for a newly indexed ASIN, rely on the selected Amazon child’s current title, structured attributes, category, and user materials for facts. State that search volume/rank is unavailable; do not attach numeric demand claims to public wording alone.
