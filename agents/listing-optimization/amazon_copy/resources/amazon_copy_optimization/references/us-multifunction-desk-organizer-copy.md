# US Multifunction Desk Organizer Copy

Use this reference for US metal/mesh desktop organizers that combine horizontal paper trays with upright file sections, pen cups, drawers, or carry handles.

## Product-fact matrix before drafting

Verify each saleable child separately and record:

- Submitted ASIN, resolved child ASIN, and parent ASIN.
- Child color and whether the family contains color-only or structural variants.
- Number of **sliding horizontal trays**.
- Number of upright file or magazine sections.
- Pen-holder count, drawer count, and handle presence.
- Metal/mesh construction and assembly requirement.
- Dimensions only after reconciling PDP details, A+ diagrams, manuals, and product comparison tables.

Do not collapse distinct counts into one vague number. A listing marketed as `5-tier` may visibly contain four sliding trays plus a usable top level; write `5-tier` only as the verified market/product structure and do not convert it into `5 sliding trays` unless the product actually has five sliding trays.

## Conflicting dimensions

Amazon PDP details, A+ graphics, comparison tables, and manuals can disagree. When dimensions conflict materially:

1. Show the conflicting values and their source modules in the audit.
2. Exclude dimensions from Title and Item Highlights.
3. Ask for product measurement or the approved packaging/manual specification before using dimensions in upload-ready copy.
4. Never silently select the most convenient value.

## Short-title allocation

Under the user's requested team format of `≤75 characters`, prioritize:

1. Brand.
2. Strongest exact product phrase.
3. One decisive structure term such as `5-Tier`, `with File Holder`, or `with Drawer`.
4. Child color when drafting a child title.

Move secondary components—magazine holder, pen cups, drawer, handle, office supplies, home/school scenarios—to Item Highlights or bullets. Avoid repeating `organizer`, `holder`, or `with` merely to preserve every old-title phrase. Programmatically count exact tokens; a product noun appearing more than twice is a title-governance risk.

## Item Highlights allocation

Use Item Highlights to complement the title with a balanced selection of:

- Metal mesh construction.
- Verified component counts: sliding trays, upright sections, pen holders, drawer.
- Access or sorting benefit.
- Representative stored objects: papers, folders, notebooks, writing tools.
- A few contexts: office, home workspace, school/classroom.

Do not turn the field into a full parts inventory. Lead with the omitted purchase-decision facts and keep the field within the requested `≤125-character` format.

## Review-informed claim gate

Use Amazon review summaries and visible review text as objection evidence, not as product specifications.

- If assembly feedback is mixed or instructions are criticized, do not use `easy assembly`, `assembles in minutes`, `labor-saving`, or similar strong convenience claims.
- It is safe to state only verified facts such as `assembly required` or `includes instructions` when supported.
- Do not promote `large capacity`, `sturdy`, or `durable` from old copy alone; qualify or omit unless supported by product facts and consistent evidence.
- Surface recurrent footprint/bulk concerns by keeping dimensions visible in images or bullets once the correct dimensions are confirmed.

## Keyword evidence labels

Current-title phrases and public competitor titles can establish market-language candidates such as:

- `desk organizer with file holder`
- `5 tier paper letter tray organizer`
- `mesh desk organizer`
- `paper organizer for desk`
- `desktop organizer with drawer`
- `paper letter tray`
- `desktop file organizer`

Do not label any phrase as a traffic term, order term, or natural-rank term without SellerSprite/SIF/SQP data or explicit user-provided keyword tiers. When those tools are unavailable, ask the user to classify core order terms, broad traffic terms, and existing natural-position terms before final drafting.

## Final checks

- Title is mapped to the correct child color and ASIN.
- `5-tier` is not misreported as five sliding trays.
- All component counts are independently verified.
- Conflicting dimensions are excluded pending confirmation.
- Assembly language reflects review evidence and does not overpromise.
- Title and Item Highlights contribute different information.
- Character counts and exact-token repetition are deterministically verified.
