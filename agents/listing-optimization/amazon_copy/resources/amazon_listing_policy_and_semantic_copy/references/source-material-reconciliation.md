These are internal training materials, not Amazon policy. Retain useful workflow ideas only after policy and evidence checks.

## Retain

- Separate creation, optimization, SEO analysis, and quality-audit workflows.
- Collect marketplace, product facts, specifications, competitor structure, keywords, and intended use before drafting.
- Extract five distinct selling points and map features to buyer benefits.
- Use competitor copy for structure and gap analysis, never for copying.
- Localize by marketplace rather than translating literally.
- Score compliance, grammar, readability, selling-point clarity, localization, and evidence quality separately.
- Map buyer questions, scenarios, attributes, proof, and benefits.
- Use reviews and Q&A as language and objection research when actual data is available.
- Compare current copy with direct competitors using evidence and source labels.

## Modify

- `TOP20` can be a maximum exploration frame, not a mandatory output. Use the number supported by the data.
- Audience, motive, concern, and review themes may be qualitative hypotheses when no data exists; never attach invented percentages.
- Keyword/root libraries should be prioritized by relevance and measured evidence, not by a fixed size.
- Q&A is an answerability plan based on verified facts, not a fixed posting cadence.
- Description and A+ should be rich in decision information, not long for its own sake.
- “Rufus” should be presented as `Alexa for Shopping（原 Rufus）`.
- A9/COSMO/Rufus scores must disclose what is measured and what is inferred.

## Reject

- Mandatory five Title keywords.
- Mandatory 20 roots and 10 keywords across bullets.
- “Embed as many keywords as possible” or keyword bolding in upload-ready copy.
- Universal 100–200-character Title templates after July 27, 2026.
- Fixed 100–150 or 200–300 character bullet targets treated as Amazon policy.
- Invented TOP20 audience/motive/review percentages.
- Claims that intent-term stuffing directly earns COSMO recommendation.
- Claims that Posts, image/video filenames, or 5–10 weekly Q&As are proven ranking factors.
- Encouraging favorable reviews, review gating, incentives, refunds, or variation abuse.
- Fabricated product benefits, accessories, medical relief, performance, or comparison claims.
- Treating AI-generated output or competitor wording as product evidence.

## Replacement operating rule

Use:

`official policy → verified product facts → measured query/behavior evidence → native-language buyer answer → deterministic field checks`

Do not use:

`fixed keyword quota → invented audience statistics → long copy → assumed algorithm reward`

## Gold-standard listing architecture (structure, not fixed copy)

When drafting upload-ready short fields, prefer this cross-category split (exemplar: natural craft shells):

1. **Title** — brand, pack/count, intact high-intent product phrase, decisive size/color; no technique/scene dump.
2. **Item Highlights** — one coherent complementary phrase (form/material + intents); do not restate full title identity.
3. **Five bullets, one intent each** — pack/fit facts → primary method/structure → scenes with restraint → projects/personalization → expectation management/care/limits.
4. **Grammar** — complete US sentences; short Title Case labels + `:`; parallel lists inside clauses; soft CTA; natural products use approximate sizes and honest variation language.
5. **Claims** — no food/heat/safety/performance upgrades without independent authorization.

Structure patterns may be reused; numbers, counts, and brand must be re-verified per ASIN.
