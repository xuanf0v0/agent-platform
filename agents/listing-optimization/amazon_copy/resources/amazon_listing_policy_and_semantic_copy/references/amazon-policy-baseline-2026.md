# Amazon Listing Policy Baseline — Post July 27, 2026

Verified: 2026-07-23. Scope: general Amazon listing creation and optimization, primarily US/EU stores. A category template, current Seller Central help page, or live field validator can impose a stricter rule and always wins.

## Hard rules and dated changes

### Title and Item Highlights

Amazon's June 10, 2026 announcement says that starting July 27, 2026:

- Titles in all categories except media must be 75 characters or fewer, including spaces.
- Item Highlights adds 125 searchable characters for materials, recommended uses, and comparison details.
- Item Highlights appears with titles in search results and on product detail pages.
- Titles still over 75 characters can be gradually replaced by Amazon AI recommendations.
- Brand owners have 14 days to review listing changes before implementation.

Source:

- https://sellercentral.amazon.com/seller-forums/discussions/t/145b6d0f-999c-4555-896c-c694bda2e470

The 2025 title policy also restricted `! $ ? _ { } ^ ¬ ¦` unless part of the brand and limited the same word to two occurrences, except prepositions, articles, and conjunctions. Keep these restrictions unless the current category guide or console validator explicitly replaces them.

Source:

- https://sellercentral.amazon.com/seller-forums/discussions/t/b2b15728-0d43-453e-974f-59eb63f73059

### Bullet points

Effective August 15, 2024, Amazon restricted special characters, emojis, and phrases such as refund-related guarantees. Amazon may remove noncompliant content and propose AI-generated compliant bullets.

Source:

- https://sellercentral.amazon.com/seller-forums/discussions/t/65f8e647-977d-49ac-9036-2049b96720b2

Amazon's May 2026 official listing guidance recommends:

- Begin each bullet with a capital letter.
- Use sentence fragments; end punctuation is not required.
- Cover features, materials, dimensions, and use cases.
- Exclude promotional, pricing, and shipping information.
- Use all five available bullets.

The same guidance says required and recommended category attributes should be complete and accurate, and the category style guide controls formatting.

Source:

- https://sellercentral.amazon.com/seller-forums/discussions/t/d37bc5d4-20d3-4a8b-ba27-ba7ab2204257

### Backend Search Terms

Amazon's May 2026 guidance says:

- Stay within 250 bytes.
- Use synonyms, abbreviations, and alternate names.
- Use lowercase with spaces and no punctuation needed.
- Do not repeat Title or brand words.
- Never include competitor brands or ASINs.
- Optimize for relevance; visible copy and backend terms work together.

Source:

- https://sellercentral.amazon.com/seller-forums/discussions/t/011cf690-5c48-484a-8b59-6058690c07b6

### Images and attributes

Amazon's May 2026 guidance states:

- Main image uses a pure white background and shows the product only, with no text, logo, or watermark.
- Product fills at least 85% of the image frame.
- Use 1000 pixels or more on the longest side for zoom.
- JPEG is preferred; TIFF and PNG are accepted.
- Secondary images show angles, lifestyle use, and key features.
- Missing or inaccurate attributes can cause suppression or poor categorization.

Source:

- https://sellercentral.amazon.com/seller-forums/discussions/t/d37bc5d4-20d3-4a8b-ba27-ba7ab2204257

## AI content and shopping-assistant readiness

Amazon's May 2026 guidance says listing content should answer common who/what/when/where/why/how questions, and complete accurate attributes improve shopping-assistant recommendations. Sellers should review listing changes regularly and dispute inaccurate changes.

Source:

- https://sellercentral.amazon.com/seller-forums/discussions/t/ba85d226-ac00-4f84-9292-27e65379c4cc

On May 13, 2026, Amazon renamed Rufus to Alexa for Shopping. The assistant uses the product catalog, customer reviews, community Q&A, shopping activity, conversational context, and information from across the web. Seller copy can improve answerability but cannot control every answer or guarantee recommendation.

Source:

- https://www.aboutamazon.com/news/retail/amazon-rufus-ai-assistant-personalized-shopping-features

## Reviews and variation integrity

Review manipulation is prohibited. Do not:

- Offer money, discounts, refunds, free products, or any incentive for a review.
- Ask for only positive reviews.
- Divert negative experiences away from Amazon while routing positive buyers to Amazon.
- Ask a reviewer to change or remove a review.
- Review one's own or a competitor's product.
- Create variations to aggregate reviews.

Use Amazon's `Request a Review` feature for neutral requests.

Sources:

- https://sellercentral.amazon.com/seller-forums/discussions/t/23858d1a9132e529477d94496803c0b4
- https://sellercentral.amazon.com/seller-forums/discussions/t/30bbc516-14c8-4162-95fe-8e119a0ddfea

From February 12 through May 31, 2026, Amazon changed review sharing across variations. Reviews continue to share for minor differences that do not affect function, including eligible color/pattern, functionally equivalent sizes, pack quantity, secondary scent, and model fitment. Functional differences can stop cross-child review sharing.

Source:

- https://sellercentral.amazon.com/seller-forums/discussions/t/a572986d-9bb1-4e91-a53f-41c575874dd7

## Policy-use notes

- A public announcement is strong evidence for the announced change, but a category template or live validator can be stricter.
- Seller Forum comments from sellers are not policy. Use the Amazon-authored announcement only.
- Do not infer missing detailed rules from a login-gated help page.
- Label official best practice separately from a hard technical limit.
- Re-verify all time-sensitive rules when a user asks for “latest,” when entering a new marketplace/category, or after a suppression/error message.
